How scores and labels work

Chess Coach estimates, not Chess.com CAPS scores. Each move scores 100 × exp(−centipawn loss / 300). A move losing no evaluation scores 100%; a 300 cp loss scores about 37%. Keeping a forced mate counts as zero loss. Side accuracy is the mean of its move scores. Book uses a small curated opening list. Great requires a clear gap to the second engine choice; Brilliant additionally requires a sound offered piece sacrifice in the principal variation. Miss means a lost forced mate or a missed forcing win. Other labels use centipawn loss: Best <20, Good <60, Inaccuracy <150, Mistake <300, otherwise Blunder. Special labels are conservative estimates at the displayed depth.
